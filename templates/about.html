About This Project

How the Soil Sustainability Score is built, validated, and used.

The Problem

Existing soil test reports provide individual parameters — pH, nitrogen, phosphorus, and so on — but don't indicate overall soil sustainability. Farmers and agriculture officers are often left interpreting raw numbers without a clear picture of soil health or what to do about it.

Our Approach

The Soil Sustainability Score (SSS) combines six chemical parameters and soil texture into a single 0–100 score using a transparent, calibrated formula:

SSS = (0.70 × Chemical Health Score) + (0.30 × Physical Health Score)

Each parameter is scored against agronomically ideal ranges, then combined with weights reflecting their relative importance to soil fertility.

Real Data, Real Validation

The system was built and tested using an actual government soil survey dataset for Akola district, Maharashtra — 112 raw records, refined down to 86 genuine soil profiles after removing non-agricultural entries (waterbodies, habitation areas).

Both machine learning models were trained on the scored dataset to confirm the formula's logic holds up — Electrical Conductivity emerged as the single most influential parameter in both models.
